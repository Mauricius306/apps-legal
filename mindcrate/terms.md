## 1. Service

Mindcrate is a task list. With an account, your tasks, subtasks and projects are

The provider works to keep the service running but does not owe uninterrupted

- You need a valid email address and a secure password that you do not share.
- An account is meant for one person.
- You can delete your account at any time in the app under *More → Delete

## 3. Your content

What you enter remains yours. You grant the provider only the rights needed to
store, sync and show it to you. Do not enter unlawful content or data about
other people that you may not use.

Keep an extra copy of what matters via *More → Import and back up*. Deleted
tasks stay in the trash for 30 days; after that they are gone for good.

## 4. Fair use

Not allowed: automated mass access, attempts to bypass protections or access
other accounts, and use that impairs the service for others. Limits apply per
account (currently 5,000 tasks, 200 projects, 100 steps per task). In case of
violations the provider may block an account after notice, and without notice
in serious cases.

responsible for what an assistant creates or changes in your list with your
permission. An assistant cannot delete anything.
